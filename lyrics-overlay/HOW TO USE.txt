LYRICS OVERLAY - shows the song's lyrics on top of your screen
================================================================

FIRST TIME ON A PC (or a PC whose copy doesn't update)
  Double-click this on the NAS:
    P:\Lyrics Overlay\Install Lyrics Overlay on a new PC.bat
  (or "Install on this PC.bat" in this folder, if you already have the folder).
  It puts a "Lyrics Overlay" shortcut on the desktop.
  If Windows asks about network access, click Allow.

EVERY DAY
  Open "Lyrics Overlay" from the desktop.
  It gets the newest version automatically before it opens.

THE PC PLAYING THE MUSIC (YouTube / Spotify)
  Right-click the lyrics bar > Sound source >
  "Follow this PC's player - auto".

EVERYONE ELSE
  Right-click the lyrics bar > Join a colleague's lyrics > pick that PC.

LYRICS A BIT EARLY / LATE?
  When you hear the small next line start, right-click > "Next line starts NOW".
  It remembers the fix for that song.

WRONG WORDS OR TIMING THAT WON'T GO AWAY?
  Right-click > "Edit this song's lyrics". Fix the words; for timing, click a line and
  press "Line starts NOW" when it's sung. Save - every PC shows your version from then on.

LYRICS IN THE VIDEO
  For YouTube videos the app reads the lyrics shown in the video itself (takes ~1 min the
  first time). Once any PC has read a video, every PC gets it instantly (kept on the NAS).
  On a fast PC, tick right-click > "Read videos ahead for the team" so the channel's other
  videos are ready before anyone plays them.

UPDATES
  The app updates itself between songs. "Update ready" on the bar = it will restart at the
  next song; right-click > Restart to do it now.
